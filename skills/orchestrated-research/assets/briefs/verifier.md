## Form of the report

First a verdict table:

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence kind | One-line reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

A verdict is PROVED, OBSERVED, REFUTED with a counterexample, or NEEDS SCOPE with the corrected statement.
Evidence kind is proof, exhaustive enumeration, or numerical sample.
PROVED needs a proof, or an exhaustive enumeration of a finite case. OBSERVED means a numerical sample supports the claim and nothing proves it.
A numerical sample can refute a general claim, but it cannot prove one.

Then, for each claim, a proof sketch and the exact computational evidence, with case counts and the commands that reproduce it.
Check domains, dimensions, quantifiers, held-fixed quantities and boundary cases.
